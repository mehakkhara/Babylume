Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Babylume is a companion app for parents of babies and toddlers. It is built and run by an individual developer, not a company. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and what control you have over it. We have tried to write it in plain language.

The short version: we store the details you type in and the photos and recordings you save, so that your journal and tips work and follow you across devices. We do not run ads, we do not sell or share your information, and we do not use analytics or tracking.

Who this policy covers

Babylume is designed for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. Information about your child is entered by you, the parent or guardian, and is treated as your information under this policy. Children do not use the app and we do not knowingly collect information directly from anyone under 13.

What we collect

Everything below is provided by you. We do not collect anything in the background.

  • Account details: your email address and a password, or a sign-in link sent to that email.
  • Your profile: your name and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Your baby's profile: name, date of birth, and optional details such as sex, feeding method, sleep arrangement, birth context, siblings and notes, used to choose which tips you see.
  • Your journal: photos, voice recordings, notes, milestones and keepsakes you save.
  • App activity: which tips you have read or saved, checklist progress, stories you have read, and mood check-ins, so the app can pick up where you left off.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks or ads.
  • No analytics or usage tracking tools.
  • No location data.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar or other apps.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of your information with anyone for their own purposes.

How your information is used

  • To show tips and activities matched to your baby's age and your preferences.
  • To keep your journal and keepsakes, and sync them between devices you sign into.
  • To sign you in, confirm your email and reset your password.
  • To send reminders you turn on. Reminders are generated on your device; we do not run a notification server.

Tips are the same for every parent in a given situation. They are not generated by, or tailored by, artificial intelligence using your data.

Where it is stored

  • On your device: your journal, profile and progress are kept in your browser's local storage so the app works offline.
  • In the cloud: the same information is backed up to Supabase, a hosting provider that handles sign-in, the database and photo storage on our behalf. Supabase acts only on our instructions and does not use your data for its own purposes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Sharing from the app: when you choose to share a keepsake or reel, the image or video is created on your device and handed to the app you pick. We never see where it goes.

Who can see it

Only you, through your account. Your journal and profile are tied to your sign-in and are not visible to other users. The developer can access the database for maintenance and support, and will only do so when needed to fix a problem or because you asked.

We will disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a person.

Your choices

  • Edit: change your profile and your baby's details any time from the Profile screen.
  • Delete entries: delete any journal entry from the app. It is removed from your device and from the cloud backup.
  • Delete your account: email us at the address below and we will delete your account and everything in it within 30 days. Account deletion inside the app is planned.
  • Export: you can save any keepsake, reel or photo to your device at any time. A full export of your data is available on request.
  • Reminders: turn them off in your device or browser notification settings.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from the live database and storage, and from backups within 30 days after that.

Security

Access to your data requires your password or a link sent to your email. The database enforces that each account can only read and write its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
